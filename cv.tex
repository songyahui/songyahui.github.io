%% ---- Research Interest -----------------------------------------------------
\section{Research Interest}
My research focuses on programming languages and program analysis, particularly
functional programming, specification and verification, static analysis, and logics
such as temporal logic and separation logic, as well as their applications in bug
finding, automated program repair, and software correctness proofs.
On the engineering side, I have a strong interest in systems programming with \Cpp,
including high-performance library design, type-safe abstractions, and applying
formal methods to real-world \Cpp\, codebases.
