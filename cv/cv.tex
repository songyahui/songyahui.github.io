\section{Summary}
  Software engineer with a Ph.D.\ in program verification and 13 peer-reviewed papers, including an
  ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper. I build \textbf{static analysis, verification and automated-repair
  tooling} that runs on real codebases: \emph{ProveNFix}, my temporal-property-guided analyser and
  repair tool, extends Meta Infer and has been evaluated on production C. Underneath it: separation
  logic, temporal logic with extended regular expressions, algebraic effects and handlers,
  and SMT solving with Z3. I am now a software developer in Standard
  Chartered's Core Strats group, building trading systems in Haskell --- a setting where correctness
  and timing carry direct financial consequence, and where I see clear potential to put these methods
  to work. Comfortable owning a problem end to end: theory, implementation, evaluation, and the
  engineering needed to get it adopted.
