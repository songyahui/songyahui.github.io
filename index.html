Yahui Song (宋雅辉)

Software Developer · Core Strats, Standard Chartered Bank

GitHub Google Scholar LinkedIn

yahui DOT song AT sc DOT com

I was a Research Fellow in the Automated Program Repair group at NUS, working with Professor Abhik Roychoudhury. I defended my PhD in May 2023 at the School of Computing, NUS, supervised by Associate Professor Wei-Ngan Chin. I received my Master's degree from NUS in 2018 and my Bachelor's degree from Sun Yat-sen University, China, in 2017.

My research is in programming languages and program analysis, covering both functional programming and object-oriented programming, verification, static analysis, and logics (temporal and separation logic), with applications to bug finding, program repair, and correctness proofs. I also have a strong interest in systems programming, including high-performance library design and applying formal methods to real-world codebases.
